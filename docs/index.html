FlyRank Search Intelligence & Content Refresh Ranking

1. The Problem

Clients manage 30,000+ content URLs with finite weekly editorial capacity (top 50 pages). Ranking pages purely on raw traffic volume or simple recency caused severe review fatigue on stable evergreen articles.

2. What I Did & The Catch

Framed as a Top-K ranking task on client-holdout splits (GroupShuffleSplit). The Catch: The obvious feature trend_pct delivered an artificial 1.000 Precision@50 in training. I caught that it was computed across the outcome measurement window—deliberate leakage. I eliminated it and built a leak-free contract using strictly pre-decision signals (impressions, striking-distance positions, update age).

3. What Came of It

Trained a leak-free Random Forest achieving Precision@50 = 0.740 on completely held-out client domains, delivering a 3.1× lift over the transparent heuristic baseline (0.240) and dataset base rate (0.542).

AI Attendance Verification & Face Recognition

1. The Problem

Manual paper roll-calls in university classrooms wasted 15% of instructional lecture time and were vulnerable to proxy attendance signing.

2. What I Did & The Catch

Built a local computer vision pipeline using OpenCV face recognition integrated with a real-time Streamlit instructor dashboard. The Catch: Default face detection failed under low lecture-hall ambient lighting. Implemented histogram equalization and multi-frame confidence gating to eliminate false registrations.

3. What Came of It

Awarded Grade A. Deployed live automated CSV attendance generation with under 1.2s recognition latency per student, cutting attendance logging time to zero.

Applied Robotics Edge AI MobileNet-SSD Sensor Fusion

Autonomous Robonova: Edge Vision & Obstacle Avoidance

1. The Problem

Default ultrasonic distance sensors on the Robonova humanoid robot experienced blind spots on low-profile and transparent obstacles, causing navigation crashes in physical arena trials.

2. What I Did & The Catch

Integrated monocular edge vision perception. The Catch: AI tools initially suggested a heavy YOLOv8 model that caused immediate thermal throttling on the robot's edge CPU (<4 FPS). Caught the trap and implemented an edge-quantized INT8 MobileNet-SSD running at 28 FPS at <15W power draw.

3. What Came of It

Achieved 94.2% obstacle detection reliability in physical arena traversal with under 35ms inference latency.

Engineering Principles & AI Rigor

Three non-negotiable rules governing how I build machine learning pipelines and use artificial intelligence.

⚖️

Transparent Baselines First

A machine learning model without an honest baseline is a number without meaning. Every model I build must beat a readable, heuristic rule score evaluated on the exact same holdout split.

🛡️

Zero Feature Leakage

Features computed over future outcome windows produce flattering offline scores that fail in production. I enforce strict time-bound data contracts prior to feature engineering.

⚡

Deliberate AI Partnership

I treat AI as an aggressive, adversarial thought partner. I verify its mathematical assertions, inspect hardware constraints, and catch plausible hallucinations before committing code.
