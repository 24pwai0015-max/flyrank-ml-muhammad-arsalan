Flagship Capstone
Search Intelligence Tabular ML Random Forest Precision@50

FlyRank Search Intelligence & Content Refresh Ranking Engine

1. The Business Problem

Clients manage 30,000+ indexed URLs with finite weekly editorial bandwidth (top 50 pages). Ranking purely on raw volume or recency wasted capacity reviewing stable evergreen URLs.

2. What I Did & The Catch

Framed as Top-K ranking on client-holdout splits (GroupShuffleSplit). The Catch: The candidate feature trend_pct yielded artificial 1.000 precision in training. I caught that it was calculated across the future outcome window. I permanently purged it and built a leak-free contract on pre-decision signals.

3. What Came of It

Trained a leak-free Random Forest achieving Precision@50 = 0.740 on held-out domains, delivering a 3.08× lift over baseline (0.240) and beating base rate (0.534).

AI Attendance Verification & Face Recognition Pipeline

1. The Problem

Logistics Delivery Time Prediction Engine

1. The Problem

Inaccurate food delivery ETAs led to customer churn and driver misallocation during peak lunch and dinner hours.

2. What I Did & The Catch

Modeled multi-variable regression on 45,000+ delivery records. The Catch: Naive distance calculation ignored weather conditions and traffic density spikes. Engineered nonlinear interaction features combining weather severity indices.

3. What Came of It

Random Forest Regressor achieved MAE = 3.4 minutes (28% improvement over linear baseline), eliminating 65% of customer support delivery delay tickets.

Engineering Rigor

How I Build Machine Learning Systems

Four non-negotiable principles that separate real engineering from academic demos.

Principle 01

Leak-Free Data Contracts

Every feature is audited against its observation timestamp. If a feature touches the outcome window, it is purged permanently, regardless of its offline metric lift.

Principle 02

Grouped Client-Holdout Splits

Random train/test splits lie when datasets span multiple domains. We evaluate strictly via GroupShuffleSplit so models prove generalizability to unseen client environments.

Principle 03

Human-Centric Action Codes

A decimal probability without context is useless to content editors. We convert probabilities and SHAP reason codes into five concrete, automated action playbooks.

Principle 04

Zero-Overhead Deployment

Static-first delivery on Edge CDNs with automated Git webhooks. Zero bloated backends, zero server bills, and sub-100ms global response latency.
